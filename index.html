🎓 Educational edition / How the maths works

The stress overlay shows approximate demand/capacity ratios. The roof is treated as simply supported spans under a stylized equivalent distributed load. For each span, M = wL²/8. The maximum moment determines the roof stress colour. Column and footing checks are simplified. Meteor impacts are converted to a game load, not a physical impact analysis. Real engineering requires dynamics, member properties, deflection, connections, buckling, foundation design, and safety factors.

A classroom edition could add teacher-created challenges, guided calculations, and downloadable scorecards. No classroom accounts or student data collection are implemented in this prototype.
